I'm Faisal

Digital Experience Design graduate from Thomas More University of Applied Sciences, based in Machelen, Belgium.

From a background in gardening to crafting digital experiences that challenge the expected, creating has always been the constant. I develop when needed, but design is where I feel most at home.

Inspired by abstract art and shaped by an unconventional sense of style, my work reflects who I am: expressive, bold, and unapologetically different. I build digital experiences that feel personal, immersive, and slightly unexpected.
