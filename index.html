ABOUT FAISAL

I am Faisal Collinet, a digital designer with a degree in Digital Experience Design, working across branding, UI/UX design, and visual systems. My work is shaped by a long-standing interest in abstract art, which pushes me to approach design in a bold and unconventional way. That influence shows in how I build structured but expressive visual systems that remain clear and functional in real-world use.

I work across digital experiences, interface design, and visual storytelling, moving from concept development to final execution. I enjoy translating ideas into coherent systems that not only look refined, but also function effectively in practice, across both digital and physical environments.

Outside of design, I draw inspiration from high-end fashion and contemporary art, regularly exploring museums and visual culture. I also have a background in garden construction and enjoy spending time in nature, which keeps my perspective grounded and balanced.

Across everything I do, I focus on clarity, intention, and strong visual direction, aiming to create work that feels both considered and impactful.
